# Novelty and limitations register

## Scope boundaries
This project concerns **stochastic queueing-network simulation of human multitasking** and **replicator-dynamics resource/attention allocation**. Do not conflate this with competitive queuing in neural action-selection literature.

## Known limitations
- Attention-check scheduling drives the tested difference between fixed and RD variants.
- Human gaze, warning awareness, and latent queue durations were not observed.
- The current model does not establish a unique causal mechanism for response times.
- The warning-responsive RD extension is a proposal, not a tested result.
- The historical holdout and model-selection procedures need careful reporting to avoid overstating generalization.

## Novelty-check rule
For every new paper, record bibliographic details, exact mechanism, dataset, overlap, remaining gap, and a qualified verdict. **Do not fabricate prior literature or claim novelty based on absence of a search.**
